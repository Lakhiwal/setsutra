Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

SetSutra is designed as a local-first workout tracker. You do not need to create an account to use the app, and Lakhiwal Labs does not operate a cloud service that stores your workout history.

This Privacy Policy explains how SetSutra, developed by Lakhiwal Labs ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the SetSutra Android application.

1. Information stored by SetSutra

SetSutra stores information that you enter into the app so that workout tracking and related features can function. Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Workout sessions and workout history
  • Exercises, sets, repetitions and weights
  • Workout routines and templates
  • Workout notes
  • Body measurements that you choose to record
  • Your locally configured profile name
  • App preferences and settings
  • Heart-rate summaries when supported wearable features are used

This information is primarily stored locally in SetSutra's private application storage on your Android device.

2. No SetSutra account is required

SetSutra does not currently require you to create an account. We do not require an email address, password, phone number, or social-media account in order to use the core workout tracking features.

3. No SetSutra workout-data server

Lakhiwal Labs does not currently operate a backend service that receives or stores your workout history, exercise logs, routines, body measurements, or profile data.

Your core workout records remain within the application's local storage unless they are transferred through one of the features or platform services described below.

4. Android backup and device transfer

SetSutra supports Android's operating-system backup and device-transfer mechanisms for eligible application data.

Depending on your Android device, Google account, operating system version and backup settings, eligible SetSutra data may be transferred by Android for encrypted cloud backup, device restoration, or device-to-device transfer.

These backups are managed by Android and Google rather than by Lakhiwal Labs. Your Android backup settings determine whether these platform backup features are enabled.

Re-downloadable cached content, such as downloaded exercise images, may be excluded from platform backups.

5. Manual backup, export and sharing

SetSutra allows you to create a portable backup of supported app data.

You may choose to save, share or copy this information. For example, you may select a document provider, cloud storage provider, another application, or the system clipboard.

These transfers occur only after you initiate the relevant action. Once data is sent to another application or storage provider, that provider's privacy practices and policies apply.

6. Wearable and heart-rate features

If you use SetSutra with compatible Wear OS functionality, workout information may be exchanged between your phone and your connected wearable.

This may include information such as workout state, exercise context, rest-timer information and heart-rate information.

SetSutra uses Google's Wearable Data Layer functionality for communication with compatible wearable devices. Depending on device connectivity and Google's platform behaviour, this communication may use Google-managed infrastructure.

Lakhiwal Labs does not use this functionality to build an advertising profile or operate a separate cloud copy of your workout history.

7. Optional online exercise images

SetSutra's core workout tracking functionality does not require downloading exercise images.

If you enable optional online exercise visuals, SetSutra may request exercise images or muscle illustrations from third-party hosting services.

These network requests may reveal ordinary connection information to those providers, such as your IP address, request time, and the requested resource.

SetSutra currently uses resources hosted through services including GitHub and wger for optional exercise visuals. Their respective privacy policies govern information handled by those services.

8. Internet access

SetSutra requests Internet access for functionality including optional online exercise imagery and supported wearable communication.

Normal workout logging, routines and locally stored history are designed to remain usable without a SetSutra-operated online account or workout database.

9. Notifications

SetSutra may request permission to display notifications, including notifications related to an active workout or rest timer.

Notification permission is optional. Denying the permission does not prevent you from using the core workout logging functionality.

10. Advertising and analytics

SetSutra does not currently contain third-party advertising.

SetSutra does not currently use product analytics, advertising analytics, behavioural tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services to collect your workout activity.

If these practices change in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or when the relevant features are introduced.

11. Sale of personal data

Lakhiwal Labs does not sell your personal information or workout information.

12. Data retention

Information stored locally by SetSutra remains on your device until you delete the relevant information, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or it is otherwise removed by the operating system.

Android backups may remain according to Google's backup and retention practices.

Copies that you manually export or share remain wherever you chose to save or send them, and SetSutra cannot automatically delete those external copies.

13. Deleting your data

Because SetSutra does not currently use a SetSutra user account or developer-operated workout-history server, there is no separate cloud account containing your workout history that must be deleted through Lakhiwal Labs.

You may delete individual supported records using the app's available delete controls.

To remove all locally stored SetSutra application data, you may clear the application's storage through Android settings or uninstall SetSutra.

Platform backups and manually exported copies must be managed through the platform or service where those copies are stored.

14. Security

SetSutra uses Android's application sandbox and private app storage for its local database and private files.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Backup files that you manually export may contain workout, measurement and profile information. You should store and share exported backup files only with destinations you trust.

15. Children's privacy

SetSutra is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

If future versions of SetSutra are intentionally directed toward children or introduce functionality that changes our obligations in this area, this policy and the application's disclosures will be updated accordingly.

16. Third-party services

Certain optional or platform-integrated functionality may rely on third-party services, including:

  • Google Android backup and device transfer
  • Google Play services / Wearable Data Layer
  • GitHub-hosted optional exercise imagery
  • wger-hosted optional exercise visual resources
  • User-selected document, sharing or cloud-storage providers

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SetSutra's features or data-handling practices change.

The effective date displayed at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
